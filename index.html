Social Dash

A personal analytics dashboard.

Social Dash is a private, single-user tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It reads the owner's YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok analytics so they can be viewed in one place. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

  • What is accessed: read-only channel/account statistics and analytics (for example views, followers, likes, and comments) for the owner's own accounts, through the official YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok APIs.
  • How it is used: only to display charts and summaries to the owner.
  • Storage: data and access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. Tokens are encrypted. Nothing is uploaded to any other server.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.
  • Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions), Instagram's Apps and Websites settings, or TikTok's Manage app permissions. Deleting the local database removes all stored data.
  • Google API data: Social Dash's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

  • Who may use it: Social Dash is operated by and for its owner only. No accounts are offered to the public.
